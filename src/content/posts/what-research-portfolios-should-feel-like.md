## Legibility first

A research portfolio is usually the first place a stranger decides whether your taste feels trustworthy.

For me, that means a few simple rules:

- show the actual work,
- say what kind of problems you care about,
- and do not bury the serious material under generic personal branding.

## Personality still matters

I do not believe professionalism requires flattening a person into neutrality. It only requires that the signal hierarchy is clear.

That is why this site keeps Home and Research professional, while Blog is allowed to be wider and more human.
