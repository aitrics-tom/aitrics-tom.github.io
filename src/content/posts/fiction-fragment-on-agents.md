It was not the first model to answer quickly.

It was the first one I trusted a little after it learned how to stop.

Not stop forever. Not refuse by habit. Just pause when the world around the task looked thinner than the task itself. That hesitation changed everything. The logs got quieter. The summaries got shorter. The human reviewers became less theatrical because there was finally something worth reading.

I still think about that fictional system because speed is the easiest thing to admire in a machine. Judgment almost always looks slower from the outside.
